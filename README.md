# Resume Roaster

Upload your resume. Get roasted. Get better.

## What is this?

Resume Roaster gives you brutally honest AI feedback on your resume. No sugarcoating, no generic advice — just real talk about what's working and what's not.

## How it works

1. **Upload** your resume (PDF)
2. **Get roasted** with honest feedback
3. **See your score** from 0-100
4. **Get suggestions** with before/after improvements
5. **Share** your roast with friends (if you dare)

## Sample Roast

**Score: 65/100**

> "Your resume is like a beige wall - technically there, but nobody's looking at it."

- Solid technical skills section
- Good use of metrics in some achievements

### The Bad
- Your summary reads like LinkedIn auto-generated it
